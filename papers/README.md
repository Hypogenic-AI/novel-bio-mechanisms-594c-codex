## Core Mechanistic Interpretability Papers

1. [InterPLM: Discovering Interpretable Features in Protein Language Models via Sparse Autoencoders](2412.12101_interplm_sparse_autoencoders.pdf)
   - Authors: Elana Simon, James Zou
   - Year: 2024 preprint; Nature Methods 2025 version
   - Why relevant: Central paper for SAE-based discovery of known and potentially novel biological concepts in ESM-2.

2. [Sparse autoencoders uncover biologically interpretable features in protein language model representations](gujral_2025_sparse_autoencoders_plm_representations.pdf)
   - Authors: Onkar Gujral, Mihir Bafna, Eric Alm, Bonnie Berger
   - Year: 2025
   - Why relevant: Unsupervised SAE/transcoder analysis of ESM2 protein-level and amino-acid-level representations with GO and LLM-assisted interpretation.

3. [From Mechanistic Interpretability to Mechanistic Biology](adams_2025_mechanistic_interpretability_to_mechanistic_biology.pdf)
   - Authors: Etowah Adams, Li Bai, Minji Lee, Yiyang Yu, Mohammed AlQuraishi
   - Year: 2025
   - Why relevant: Explicitly frames SAE features as hypotheses for unknown biological mechanisms and provides InterProt tooling.

4. [MotifAE Reveals Functional Motifs from Protein Language Model](hou_2025_motifae.pdf)
   - Authors: Chao Hou, Di Liu, Yufeng Shen
   - Year: 2025
   - Why relevant: SAE variant with local smoothness loss for unsupervised functional motif discovery from ESM2.

5. [Protein Circuit Tracing via Cross-layer Transcoders](2602.12026_protein_circuit_tracing_cross_layer_transcoders.pdf)
   - Authors: Darin Tsui, Kunal Talreja, Daniel Saeedi, Amirali Aghazadeh
   - Year: 2026
   - Why relevant: Directly addresses cross-layer circuits in PLMs and compressed circuits for function prediction.
